Time Trial Spectator Mode 0.3.0
Author: Onur

Watch Time Trial ghosts like a replay: follow any ghost, switch between
ghosts, pause, rewind, fast forward or slow down.

HOW TO USE
In Time Trial, open the ghost menu of a track and choose the ghosts as usual.
Then press L1 (Spectate) instead of Continue, and Ready. The race starts
without you: the camera follows a ghost.
  L1 / R1               previous / next ghost
  Square                pause / play
  D-pad left / right    hold: rewind / fast forward (x4)
  D-pad up / down       speed: x0.1, x0.5, x1, x2, x3, x4
  Triangle              show / hide the controller
  Options               the game's pause menu (Restart, Quit)

WHAT YOU SEE AND HEAR
- The ghost's time, name, number and the playback speed (top left).
- A controller with the ghost's inputs and steering.
- The watched ghost looks like a normal kart; your kart and your minimap icon
  are hidden.
- The watched ghost sounds like your own kart: engine, drifts, boosts, jumps,
  surfaces, water, boost pads and the character's voice lines, with the
  game's own rules. Wall hits play the impact and the "Ouch" line. The other
  ghosts stay silent.
- The run stops at the finish and can be rewound. Nothing is saved: no ghost,
  no unlocks.

Works together with Ghost Sharing (shared ghosts can be watched too).

LIMITS
- Ghost files do not keep collisions: wall hits are found from the movement
  with the game's own rule, so a rare hit may differ from the race.
- One local player.

GAME: Crash Team Racing Nitro-Fueled, USA (CUSA13795) version 1.21, OCTR
launcher with code-signature support. The launcher checks the game code before
it applies the patch and refuses it on any other game build.

INSTALL: Mods > Add files, choose this ZIP, enable it, fully restart the game.
UNINSTALL: disable or remove it.
